# Touch screens (phones and tablets) — plan

Status: **Figma drawn and decided; O-1, O-2 and O-3 done (not yet tried on a phone)** (2026-10-12). Section « O · Tactile » is on « Editor —
Behaviors » (node 250:11573): O0 six rules, O1 browse and arrange (4 phone steps), O2 long-press, lasso, selection mode
and tap-to-explain (4 steps), O3 cases, O4 decisions. Next: O-4.

Many first visits will come from a phone. Today the Bureau is built for a mouse and a keyboard; this plan lists what
breaks on touch and proposes the behaviour to draw before coding (same route as K–N).

## Audit (code as of `feat/qol`)

| Area | Today | On touch |
|---|---|---|
| Desk pan | Wheel, middle button, Space + drag (`DeskCanvas` `onPointerDown`) | One finger on empty desk draws a **lasso**; there is no way to pan with a finger. |
| Desk zoom | Ctrl/⌘ + wheel, trackpad pinch (wheel events), `+`/`−` | No pinch: a second finger is ignored (`gesture.pointerId` check); `touch-action: none` also blocks the browser's zoom. |
| Move a paper | Drag the paper; since `feat/qol` also the grip on the context bar | Works with one finger, but a finger landing on a paper to scroll moves it instead. |
| Select several | Shift/Ctrl + click, lasso | Lasso only (conflicts with pan); no modifier keys. |
| Open | Double-click, Entrée, « Ouvrir » | Double-tap works in most browsers; « Ouvrir » in the bar works. |
| Context menu | Right-click | Android long-press fires `contextmenu`; **iOS Safari does not**. |
| Resize | 8 handles a few px wide | Too small to hit; no `pointer: coarse` sizing. |
| Tooltips (`WithTooltip`) | Hover / focus | Never shown; « unavailable » reasons are invisible on touch. |
| Context bar | Floats above the selection | Can cover the paper under the finger; wide bars overflow a 360 px screen (AdaptiveToolbar folds below 600 px, the desk bar does not). |
| Editors (docs, sheets, slides) | Narrow-width CSS exists for docs and sheets | Not reviewed on a phone: on-screen keyboard resizing the viewport, cell selection by touch, slide editing. |
| Shortcuts | Many (`shortcuts.ts`) | Unreachable; every action needs a visible way in. |

## Proposed behaviour (to draw in Figma, section O)

- **O1 · Gestures on the desk.** One finger on empty desk = pan; two fingers = pinch-zoom and pan; lasso = long-press
  on empty desk, then drag (or a « Sélectionner » mode in the bottom bar). One finger on a paper = drag only after a
  short hold (~150 ms) or once it is selected; a quick swipe over a paper pans.
- **O2 · Selection.** Tap = select; tap another paper while « Sélection multiple » is on (bottom-bar toggle) = add.
- **O3 · Long-press = context menu** everywhere (our own timer, since iOS sends no `contextmenu`), with a small haptic
  (`navigator.vibrate` where available).
- **O4 · Context bar on a phone.** Docked at the bottom above the bottom bar instead of floating (J6 already allows a
  docked bar), with the grip kept on the hand side; overflow into « Plus ».
- **O5 · Targets.** 44 px minimum under `pointer: coarse` (resize handles become corner tabs, swatches, tools).
- **O6 · Explanations without hover.** Unavailable tools explain on tap (toast or popover) instead of a tooltip.
- **O7 · Phone layout of the Bureau.** Bottom bar, menu, minimap, welcome at 360–430 px; list view as the default
  project view on phones?
- **O8 · Editors.** Per editor: reading first (scroll, zoom), then editing; on-screen keyboard and `visualViewport`.

## Decisions (2026-10-12, Figma O4)

1. **Lasso:** both — long-press on empty desk then drag, and a « Sélection » mode in the bottom bar (the visible way in).
2. **Phone default view:** the desk, zoomed to « Tout voir »; the list stays in Affichage and is remembered if chosen.
3. **Editing on phones (for now):** read, write notes, arrange and present on phones; editing documents, spreadsheets
   and presentations from tablets up. Editing tools stay visible with their reason on phones.
4. **Hold before a paper follows the finger:** 150 ms, or none if the paper is already selected (and none on the grip).
5. **Bottom bar on phones:** icon-only; names stay as accessible labels and show on long-press; labels stay on tablets
   and desktops.

## Phases (after Figma)

- [x] O-1 `@oel/desk`: touch-only path in `DeskCanvas` — one finger on empty desk pans (a tap clears the selection),
  two fingers pinch (`pinchViewport` / `store.pinch`, tested), an unselected paper follows after 150 ms (a swipe first
  pans), a selected paper or the grip at once, double tap opens, a second finger during a move is ignored. Not yet:
  pan inertia, two-finger double tap = « Tout voir ».
- [x] O-2 `@oel/desk` + `apps/web`: a finger held 500 ms on a paper opens its menu (`armLongPress`, short vibration;
  the drag it had started is dropped — Figma O2 · 1 says the menu closes if the finger then moves, not done: the
  menu simply stays); held on empty desk it starts a lasso (decision 1, so empty-desk long-press is not a menu,
  unlike rule O0 · 4); Android's own `contextmenu` from a finger is ignored. `DeskCanvas` `multiSelect` + bottom-bar
  « Sélection » (count, Ranger en ligne, Supprimer, OK; Échap leaves; the context bar hides meanwhile). Bottom bar
  icon-only under 600 px (labels kept for screen readers).
- [x] O-3 `@oel/emboss` + `@oel/desk`: under `pointer: coarse` every kit control is 44 px (`styles/coarse.css`),
  and so are the bottom bar's round ends; the desk's eight resize handles become four corner tabs (24 px shown,
  44 px hit). `WithTooltip` on touch: a tap on an unavailable tool opens its slip, a 500 ms hold on any tool opens
  it without acting (names of the icon-only phone bar, decision 5; short vibration), the next touch closes it.
  Dialogs no longer overflow a phone (overlay grid column `minmax(0, 1fr)`). Phone bar hides Trier and Étaler
  (not built yet) as in Figma O1, so 44 px keys fit 360 px. Checked in Chromium phone emulation (390 × 844).
  Not done: a hold on a trigger driven from outside (« + » menu, Importer's file picker) shows the name but still
  acts on release; some unavailable reasons speak of keys (« Alt + F1 ») that a phone doesn't have.
- O-4 `apps/web`: docked context bar and phone layout of the chrome.
  Seen in emulation: the desk hint card wraps one word per line and sits under the zoom pill; the context bar
  floats over the papers.
- O-5 Editors, one per step (docs, sheets, slides), reading first.

Testing: a real phone on the LAN (`npm run dev -- --host`) plus Firefox/Chrome device emulation; the touch gestures
can't be fully checked with emulation.
